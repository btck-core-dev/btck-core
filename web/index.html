Official Pool Policy · Anti-Hopping

Continuous contribution protects pool-side Combo eligibility

POLICY PREVIEW · GUARDIAN ACCESS ONLY

Regular block income and Combo attribution are separate. Normal PPLNS income is allocated by accepted-share difficulty and the published pool-fee schedule. If this official pool's on-chain coinbase wins a Combo, the pool attributes that Combo entitlement to one qualified worker identity under the rules below and credits each tranche only after its consensus unlock height.

1 · Qualification is measured from accepted shares

A worker identity is the exact wallet-address and worker-name pair. It qualifies only when at least 95% of the epoch's slots contain an accepted share and the stage-specific consecutive-miss limit is not exceeded. This ledger rule does not prove a distinct physical machine.

2 · The same future beacon selects attribution

If the original pool worker is qualified, its winning block remains the attributed block. If not, the pool re-applies the same committed future beacon to blocks contributed by qualified official-pool workers and selects the nearest XOR candidate.

3 · PPLNS income is not forfeited

Failing the Combo gate removes only official-pool Combo eligibility. Valid accepted shares remain eligible for ordinary PPLNS accounting. If the epoch has no qualified pool candidate, its Combo remains undistributed rather than being promised to an ineligible worker.
