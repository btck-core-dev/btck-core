Guardian-phase disclosure

The first 271,828 blocks were produced with controlled project hashrate to reduce cold-start majority-hashrate risk. Genesis created no spendable reward, but the Guardian phase produced valid consensus rewards under the published 80/15/5 allocation. Bitcoin K therefore does not claim that mining was publicly open from block zero.
