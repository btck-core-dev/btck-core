The domain route has a historical long-connection fault still under investigation. Miners stable on the direct IP should keep their current endpoint. Domain and IP reach the same server; they are not independent backup pools.

Choose a mode and difficulty tier

SOLO ordinary block income goes directly to the finder under the published fee-block policy. PPLNS allocates ordinary income by accepted share difficulty over a rolling 10,000-share window. It reduces individual block variance without guaranteeing income. Both modes must remain visible regardless of hashrate.

Tier ranges guide initial share frequency, not admission. For intermediate rates, start with the adjacent lower tier and observe accepted shares. VarDiff then adjusts. The node target and ASIC firmware floor may override initial tiers. A higher port does not improve reward probability. 1 TH/s = 1,000 GH/s.

Miner settings

Worker: <your BTC-K wallet address>.<unique machine name>. Password: x. Never enter a wallet unlock password. The port selects mode. Keep failover endpoints in the same mode unless deliberately switching.

Fees and payment status

The configured fee schedule is 5% on launch days 1–7, 3% on days 8–30 and 1% afterward. PPLNS is configured for a 5 BTC-K minimum, UTC batches at 00:00/06:00/12:00/18:00, mature-input filtering and six-confirmation settlement. These are configured rules, not active payment promises: payouts remain disabled. Existing unpaid balances remain recorded.

HTTP monitoring ports 3337/3338 are loopback-only; node RPC 16110 and P2P 16111 are not mining ports.
